# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 2, 2026

Thank you for using **Cowdlly Utilities**.

# Information We Collect

**Cowdlly Utilities does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.**

The application:

- Does not require an account
- Does not require sign in
- Does not collect your name, email, or phone number
- Does not collect your location
- Does not collect device identifiers
- Does not collect contacts, photos, or files
- Does not track your activity

Everything runs locally on your device.

---

# Local Data Storage

To improve your experience, the application stores a small amount of information locally on your device using SharedPreferences.

This includes:

- Theme preference (Light, Dark, or System)
- Favorite tools
- Recently used tools
- Random number history

This information never leaves your device and is never shared with us or any third party.

# Internet Usage

The application is designed to work completely offline.

It does not communicate with any servers and does not upload or download user information.
